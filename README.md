# Road Side Buddy


Road Side Buddy is a web application designed to provide quick and affordable roadside assistance services. Whether you have a flat tire, a dead battery, a lockout, or need fuel delivery, Road Side Buddy connects you to help at competitive prices.

Features
Request tire service, battery service, lockout assistance, or fuel delivery quickly and conveniently.
Track your service history and access detailed records of past requests.
Secure user authentication for seamless login and account management.
Admin controls to monitor services, manage users, and ensure platform integrity.
How to Use Road Side Buddy
1. Access the Web App
You can access Road Side Buddy on any device by visiting:
🔗 Road Side Buddy

2. Sign Up or Log In
If you are a new user, sign up for an account.
If you already have an account, log in to proceed.
3. Request a Service
Once logged in, you will be redirected to the home page.
Select the service you need (Tire, Battery, Lockout, or Fuel Delivery).
Enter your location and submit your request.
Checking Your Service History
Navigate to the Home Page and click on the "History" tab in the top menu.
View a list of past service requests.
Click on any service to see detailed information.
Managing Your Account
Edit your profile and password from the account settings at any time.
Admins can monitor services, manage users, delete services, and block accounts when necessary.
